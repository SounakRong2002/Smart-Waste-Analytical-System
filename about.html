About Smart Waste Analytical System

A data-driven platform for understanding global food wastage.

OUR PURPOSE

Making Food Waste Data Easier to Understand

Smart Waste Analytical System is designed to provide a centralized platform for analyzing global food wastage data.

The system focuses on transforming raw datasets into meaningful analytical information through statistical analysis, data visualization and comparative analysis.

Django will provide the backend infrastructure for managing datasets, analytical results, users and dynamic website functionality.

📊

Data → Analysis → Insight

Our approach transforms raw food waste data into understandable visual insights.
